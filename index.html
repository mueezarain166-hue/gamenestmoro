Welcome to Game Nest Moro

Game Nest is a modern gaming zone in Golden Market, Moro, made for friends and gamers who want more than a game at home. Bring your crew, pick your platform and play in a space that is comfortable, lively and built around the fun of gaming together.

From console gaming on PS4 and Xbox to a steering-wheel racing setup, every station delivers a premium gaming experience with large displays, comfortable seating and an RGB atmosphere. Whether you are here for a relaxed match or a competitive showdown, our friendly environment and affordable packages make it easy to jump in and play.
